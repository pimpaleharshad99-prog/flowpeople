Security & privacy

Biometric data deserves the highest care

FlowPeople is built so your people's data stays protected, private and under your control.

Encrypted end to end

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Biometric templates are stored as encrypted math, not as photos.

Role-based access

Decide exactly who can see salaries, documents and attendance. Every permission is yours to set.

Full audit trail

Every change to a record is logged with who, what and when, ready for audits.

Consent and privacy controls

Record employee consent for biometric capture, set retention periods and delete data on request.

Reliable cloud

Daily backups and a resilient setup keep your records safe and available.

Anti-spoofing

Liveness checks and device binding help make sure the person at the kiosk is really them.

Biometric data is used only for attendance and is never used for advertising or sold to third parties.

FAQ

Questions customers ask us

How does face recognition attendance work?

Employees enrol once with a short face capture. After that, they look at a tablet or kiosk and the system matches them, checks liveness and records the punch in about two seconds.

Do you store photos of my employees' faces?

FlowPeople stores an encrypted biometric template instead of a usable photo. You control retention and can delete a person's data at any time.

Can we use fingerprint devices as well?

Yes. You can use face, fingerprint or both, and mix methods across locations. All punches appear in the same attendance records.

Does attendance connect to payroll?

Yes. Attendance, overtime and approved leave flow into payroll automatically, so there is no copying between files.

Can employees use it on their phones?

Yes. Employee self-service lets people view payslips, apply for leave and request attendance fixes from their phone or laptop.

How long does setup take?

Most teams are live within a day. You import employees from a spreadsheet, set policies and start enrolling faces or fingerprints.

Is there a free trial?

Yes, 14 days free with no credit card needed. Start your free trial.
